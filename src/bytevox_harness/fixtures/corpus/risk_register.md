# North Harbor Bus Pilot: Risk Register (fictional demonstration data)

## data-handling
The pilot used aggregate passenger counts from infrared door sensors. It did not collect names, payment identifiers, raw GPS trails, or device identifiers. Aggregate counts are retained for 30 days, then deleted. Access is limited to the transit analytics team.

## remaining-risk
The final-week survey had 46 responses and was not representative of all riders. The report flags uncertainty about whether the new boarding process works equally well at crowded stops for wheelchair users. The next phase should include direct accessibility review before expansion.
