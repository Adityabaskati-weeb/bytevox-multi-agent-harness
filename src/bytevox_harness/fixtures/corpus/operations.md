# North Harbor Bus Pilot (fictional demonstration data)

## pilot
The North Harbor pilot ran on routes 4 and 7 for 90 days, from April through June 2026. It used eight buses and covered three high-volume stops. The baseline on-time rate was 82 percent.

## delay-causes
Dispatch tagged 38 percent of recorded delays to long boarding queues, 27 percent to bridge openings, and 18 percent to operator handoffs. The remaining 17 percent had mixed or unknown causes. The tags came from 420 delayed trips.

## interventions
The team trialed all-door boarding at the three busiest stops, added a four-minute schedule buffer before the bridge crossing, and introduced a signed handoff checklist for operators. These were operational changes; no passenger-level location history was collected.

## results
The on-time rate rose from 82 percent at baseline to 88 percent during the final four weeks. All-door boarding reduced median dwell time by 22 seconds at the three pilot stops. Bridge-related delays fell from 27 percent to 19 percent of tagged delays. The pilot was not randomized, so the report does not claim that the interventions alone caused the improvement.
