# North Harbor Bus Pilot: Incident Review (fictional demonstration data)

## ramp-incident
On May 18, one bus ramp stopped halfway through deployment during boarding. No one was injured. The driver used the accessible spare bus procedure, and dispatch sent a replacement vehicle 11 minutes after the report.

## follow-up
The review found that the monthly maintenance checklist did not include a loaded ramp sensor check. The checklist was updated on May 25, and mechanics inspected all eight pilot buses by May 30. One sensor needed adjustment; the other seven passed.

## service-continuity
The review recommends keeping the spare bus procedure and recording dispatch response time for future ramp reports. It does not establish a fleet-wide ramp failure rate because the pilot covered only eight buses and 90 days.
